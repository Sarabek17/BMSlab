      Engagement models

      Pick how we work together.

      Every model starts the same way: a clear scope, a named team and a weekly demo.

      01 / 04

      Your own product team, fully run by us.

      A cross-functional squad — developers, QA, a tech lead and design if needed — working on your product only. You own the roadmap; we own delivery and team health.

      Discuss this model
      Best for
      Long-term products and roadmaps of six months or more.
      How it works
      • Team assembled for your stack and seniority mix
      • Weekly demos, sprint reports and shared metrics
      • Scale up or down with 30 days’ notice
      Billing
      Monthly fee per team member.
      02 / 04

      Senior engineers who join your team.

      Extend your in-house team with individual specialists. They work inside your processes, tools and hours — just like your own hires.

      Discuss this model
      Best for
      Closing skill gaps fast without growing headcount.
      How it works
      • Shortlisted CVs within days; you run the interviews
      • Engineers attend your stand-ups and code reviews
      • Free replacement if the fit is not right
      Billing
      Monthly rate per engineer, no hiring fees.
      03 / 04

      A defined result for a defined budget.

      We agree on scope, milestones and acceptance criteria up front, then deliver in vertical slices you can see working every week.

      Discuss this model
      Best for
      MVPs, redesigns, integrations and well-scoped features.
      How it works
      • Discovery and a written specification first
      • Fixed price per milestone
      • Warranty period after launch
      Billing
      Fixed price, paid by milestone.
      04 / 04

      From idea to a working AI pilot in 30 days.

      We pick one high-value use case, connect it to your real data and ship a pilot your team can test — with measured quality, not a demo video.

      Discuss this model
      Best for
      Teams exploring LLMs, assistants and automation.
      How it works
      • Week 1: use case, data and success metrics
      • Weeks 2–3: build, evaluate, iterate
      • Week 4: pilot in production and a go/no-go report
      Billing
      Fixed price for the pilot.
